---
title: Accessibility
description: Big targets, readable memories, states that never rely on colour, and nothing that plays by surprise.
---

## Targets and text

- Tap targets are at least 48px. Choices are 56px, or 64px on iPad. Continue is 60px.
- Memory text is 20px with a 1.6 line height on iPad, and 17px on phones.

## Never colour alone

Every state has a second signal. Glyphs on choices, cuts on gems, and the sound state spelled out in words.

## Motion and focus

- Reduced motion collapses all motion. Card flips become swaps.
- Keyboard focus shows a 4px gold outline.

## Real controls

Use real buttons and inputs, and the proper switch and radio roles.

## Sound

Nothing plays before the deliberate Ready tap. The sound toggle is always within reach.

Storybook runs automatic accessibility checks on every story. The full rules are in `guidelines/a11y.html`.
