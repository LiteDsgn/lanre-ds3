## The game frame

The design frame is a 390 × 844 portrait phone.

- A fixed **56px HUD strip** runs across the top. The level star and XP sit on the left, the currency pills and gear on the right.
- The **world scrolls** underneath it.
- An **88px wood bottom bar** holds four captioned square icon buttons.
- **Side rails** hold captioned icon buttons, stacked 14 to 16px apart.
- **Panels** are centred and at most 340px wide. They're never edge to edge, and they sit over the `--bg-scrim` veil.
- The **inventory** is 4 columns of 72px cells with 8px gaps. The **reward calendar** is 3 columns.

The Birthday Adventure swaps the wood bottom bar for its own toolbar. See [The toolbar](/docs/adventure/map#the-toolbar).

## Birthday Adventure layouts

The recipient’s experience is iPad-first, with phones supported. The contributor flow is phone-first. These sizes are proposed, not approved.

- iPad portrait is 820 wide, landscape 1180. Phones are 390.
- The reading column is at most 640.
- Panels on iPad are at most 560.
- Design portrait-first. Landscape can place media beside the text.
