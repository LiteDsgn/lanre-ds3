Label chip: merchandising and status labels; 1–3 uppercase words.

```jsx
<Tag>Most popular</Tag>
<Tag tone="teal">3x the value</Tag>
<Tag tone="gold" size="sm">New</Tag>
<Tag tone="stone" icon={<i className="ph-fill ph-lock" />}>Locked</Tag>
```

- danger (coral) for scarcity, teal/green for value, gold for new/featured, berry for premium, stone for locked.
