- Eight questions, one point each, and no timer.
- The first completed attempt is the one that counts.
- Ties share a rank, shown as "=3".
- Anyone who skips holds a pearl and isn't ranked.
- The leaderboard is for friends only. Babe never sees it.
- The copy celebrates taking part, not winning.

See [Gemstones](/docs/adventure/gemstones) for how scores become gems.
