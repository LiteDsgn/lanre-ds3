Lanre
Design system for Lanre’s Birthday Adventure

A tactile, painted UI language for a winding-road memory game: four themed worlds and a summit, mystery levels with mixed media, identity reveals, gemstones, side-of-road discoveries, plus the phone-first flow friends use to contribute. Everything pressable has a solid bottom edge; everything on the world wears an outline.

01

Principles

Four rules produce the look. Follow them and a screen reads as Lanre even before any art lands.

Tactile

Every pressable is a bevel: 300 highlight on top, 500 face, a hard 700 bottom edge, brown drop below. Pressing travels 4px down and collapses the edge. Tracks and cells are the inverse, inset wells.

Outlined

Text and glyphs that sit directly on the world carry a dark stroke painted behind the fill. Plain text lives only inside panels. The stroke tone matches the surface.

Painted

Saturated oklch ramps, warm brown ink, brown-tinted shadows, white rings on badges and tokens. No greys, no hairlines, no blur, no photos.

Warm & brief

The game talks to one player in short imperatives, CLAIM, PLAY, GO, and lets numbers do the talking: +500, 3/6, 00:02. No emoji, ever.

02

Live screens

The UI kit is a working shell composed only from the components below. Play it: it is the fastest way to learn how the pieces sit together.

Try these flows

  • 1Map → level. Tap the pulsing blue puck (or the Level CTA) → Play → the ribbon Level Complete panel with popping stars; progress advances.
  • 2Daily reward. Gift button top-right → wooden-sign panel → Claim advances the streak and fires a toast.
  • 3Trail. GO rolls, the token bounces to the tile, energy/points update; reward and hazard tiles toast.
  • 4Camp. Tap two matching items to merge; tap an item then an empty cell to move it.
  • 5Shop & settings. Coin/gem “+” opens the shop; the gear opens switches, sliders, language.

Layout rules

56px fixed HUD (level star + XP left, currency pills right, gear). World scrolls beneath. Side rails hold captioned square icon buttons. 88px wood bottom bar with 4 tabs. Panels centred, ≤340px, over a 55% scrim. One lg CTA per screen.

03

Color

Eight tones, each a five-step oklch ramp with one recipe everywhere: 500 is the face, 300 the top highlight, 700 the bevel or side, 900 the outline. Locked or disabled things swap their whole tone for Stone.

Semantic aliases

Components read aliases, not ramps, retint the game by remapping these in tokens/colors.css.

Environments & surfaces

Spacing & layout

4px base. Touch targets never under 48px; the primary CTA is 60px. Inventory grids are 4 × 72px with 8px gaps; reward calendars are 3 columns.

Space scale

Screen anatomy: HUD, world, bottom bar, panel width
Sizes: glyphs 20/28/40/64, pill 36, touch 48/60, puck 84, cell 72
06

Depth & effects

Depth is drawn, not blurred. Bevels for pressables, inset wells for tracks and cells, brown-tinted drops, glow rings for the tappable or rewarded thing. Corners are always rounded; pucks and blobs are ellipses.

Bevel & press: 3 / 5 / 7px edges, 4px travel
Shadows & glows: warm drops, tone rings
Radii: 6 tags · 10 badges/knobs · 16 buttons/cells · 22 rows/tabs · 28 panels · pill tracks
/* the bevel recipe */
background: linear-gradient(180deg, var(--gold-300) 0%, var(--gold-500) 42%);
box-shadow: 0 var(--bevel-md) 0 var(--gold-700),            /* hard bottom edge */
            0 11px 14px oklch(0.2 0.04 60 / .25),          /* warm drop */
            inset 0 2px 0 oklch(1 0 0 / .45);              /* top gloss */
/* pressed: transform: translateY(4px); edge collapses to 1px; 120ms var(--ease-out). hover: filter: brightness(1.06) only */
07

Motion

Arrivals bounce, presses ease out, claimable things float, the current thing pulses. Keyframes ship in tokens/utilities.css.

  • --ease-bounce cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) · --dur-slow 420ms: mp-pop for stars, toasts, panels
  • --ease-out · --dur-fast 120ms: press travel, tab switches, --dur-base 220ms for toggles
  • mp-float 2400ms: idle bob for available rewards · mp-pulse-ring 1600ms: current level, reachable tile · mp-sparkle, mp-shimmer, accents
  • Progress fills animate width over 420ms. No fade-only transitions, no parallax.
08

Iconography

Phosphor Icons from CDN: FILL weight for pictorial glyphs, BOLD for utility strokes, wrapped by GameIcon, which adds tone, a dark outline behind the fill and a hard drop so every glyph reads like a sticker. Semantic kinds lock glyph and tone together so currencies look identical on every screen.

<GameIcon kind="coin" size={32} />                       // gold coin
<GameIcon kind="gem" /> <GameIcon kind="heart" size={20} />  // blue gem · coral heart
<GameIcon name="anchor" tone="blue" size={40} />          // any Phosphor glyph
<GameIcon kind="gear" outline={false} shadow={false} />   // plain glyph inside a bevelled button
  • Sizes 20 / 28 / 40 / 56. Inside buttons: white, no outline, no shadow.
  • Currency tones are fixed: coin gold · gem blue · ruby/heart coral · energy teal · gift berry · lock stone.
  • No emoji, no unicode glyphs, no stroke-only icons on the world. Painted PNG/SVG art can replace glyphs later without changing call sites.
09

Worlds

The road runs through four contributor worlds to the summit. Each world is a data-world scope in tokens/worlds.css: sky → ground gradient, an accent for signs and chips, a path colour, and the stroke tone for outlined text. Level numbering is continuous across worlds; uneven populations are fine and never padded.

World chips (contributor theme picker)

<section data-world="japan" class="mp-world">…</section>      /* everything inside reads --world-* */
<WorldChip world="small_chops" showBlurb selected={theme === 'small_chops'} onClick={…} />
  • Contributor prompts are inspiration, not categories; each package still gets exactly one world for placement. The summit is never a choice.
  • Scenery per world (painted art, in slots): mango orchard · rice, restaurants, stalls · green hills with workout details · lanterns, gardens, distant mountain · golden hour above the clouds.
10

Identity & gems

Gemstone, Avatar, RevealCard. Quiz scores map to a stone: 0–2 Amber, 3–5 Emerald, 6–8 Amethyst (tier I / II / III within each), quiz skipped → Pearl keepsake, unranked. Cut and name accompany colour, so the stone never relies on colour alone.

Avatars

const { gem, tier } = gemForScore(score);     // null → { gem: 'pearl', tier: 0 }
<Gemstone gem={gem} tier={tier} size={64} />                     // Babe: stone only
<Gemstone gem={gem} tier={tier} size={84} showTier />            // friends: "Emerald II"
<Avatar name="Ada Okafor" seed={contributor.id} src={portrait} />  <Avatar mystery />

Babe's reveal card shows

  • Portrait or generated avatar, name Lanre knows, relationship
  • The gemstone as a picture (tap to learn what gems are)
  • The rest of the package after the reveal

Never on Babe's surfaces

  • Score, rank or tier text, anywhere, including Your People
  • Leaderboard, "who loves her most" framing, crowns
  • Any identifying data for people not yet revealed
11

Mystery level

The core loop, composed from the kit: PackageStepper walks the package in the contributor's order; MemoryText, PhotoStack, AudioPlayer, VideoFrame show it; ChoiceButtons ask who sent it (up to three real names); RevealCard flips; an explicit Continue completes the level. Nothing auto-advances and affection is never gated behind a right answer.

// one level, one persisted interaction: 'guess' | 'match' | 'direct'
<PackageStepper items={pkg.items} current={i} seen={seen} onSelect={setI} />
{item.type === 'text'  && <MemoryText><p>{item.text}</p></MemoryText>}
{item.type === 'photo' && <PhotoStack photos={item.photos} />}
{item.type === 'audio' && <AudioPlayer src={item.url} title="Voice note" />}
{options.map((o, k) => <ChoiceButton index={k} state={stateFor(o)} onClick={() => guess(o)}>{o.name}</ChoiceButton>)}
<Button variant="ghost" typeface="ui">Just tell me</Button>
<RevealCard revealed={revealed} {...sender} />   <Button variant="confirm" size="lg">Continue</Button>

Media pieces

  • Media titles are neutral ("Voice note", "Photo 2"); filenames never reach Babe's payload. Text is verbatim, 20px on iPad, ≤ 640px column.
  • Wrong guess → that option turns coral with ×, the rest stay live, retry and "Just tell me" remain. Correct → green with ✓, others dim, reveal flips.
  • Self-revealing media (face, spoken name) skips the guess: matching game or direct reveal, chosen once and persisted.
  • Completed levels stay replayable; refresh mid-level restores position.
12

Map extras

WorldGate signposts each world boundary (with Henry's authored note when there is one) and locks the summit until the main path is done. DiscoveryMarkers sit beside the road as thematic objects, optional, never required, and "New surprise" when a late submission arrives after the route was frozen.

<WorldGate world="good_energy" levels="Levels 12–16" note={henry.notes.good_energy} />
<WorldGate world="summit" locked={!mainPathComplete} />
<DiscoveryMarker kind="dumbbell" state={found ? 'opened' : late ? 'new' : hidden ? 'hidden' : 'available'} onClick={open} />
  • The route is frozen at first start: later packages and people appear as discoveries, never as new numbered levels.
  • Use the base LevelNode for numbered levels (blue current with pulse; complete keeps stars off: this game has no star scores, so pass stars={0} or hide the row).
13

Matching game

MatchCard, a brief, skippable pairs game for levels whose media already gives the sender away. Symbols come from Lanre's world: mango, rice bowl, flower, dumbbell, lantern.

  • 3 pairs on phones, 4 on iPad. Skip is always visible; finishing is a flourish, not a requirement.
  • Pick the interaction per level once and persist it, never re-roll on render.
14

Your People

PersonTile builds the collection: ranked people ordered by quiz result but shown as gems, not numbers; unrevealed people are neutral mystery entries; late arrivals wait as "New" until their discovery reveal.

  • At main-path completion all main-path people are revealed; discovery people still need their own reveal.
  • Grid: 3 per row on phones, 5–6 on iPad; the world background shows through mystery tiles.
15

Contributor flow

Phone-first, no account. StepBar → TextFields → composer with UploadTiles in the order Babe will see → WorldChip theme → RadioCard identity suitability → preview → submit → optional quiz. Save/resume via a private edit link; no duplicates on return.

<StepBar steps={['You', 'Memory', 'Theme', 'Preview', 'Submit']} current={step} onSelect={goTo} compact />
<UploadTile type="video" state="uploading" progress={p} onRemove={cancel} />   <UploadTile type="photo" state="error" onRetry={retry} />
<RadioCard checked={!reveals} title="Keeps me a mystery" description="No face, name or voice in here." … />
  • Say plainly that this is a surprise and that the content will be shown to Lanre; show what stays anonymous vs. what is revealed (name, relationship, portrait only after the reveal).
  • Never promise automatic anonymisation. A failed upload blocks submission until fixed or removed. One package per contributor is marked as the main-path memory; others become discoveries.
16

Quiz & leaderboard

Friends only. Eight multiple-choice questions (ChoiceButton), one point each, no timer; the first completed attempt is the ranked result. LeaderboardRow shows rank (ties share), gem + tier and score; skipped players hold a pearl and sit unranked, never as 0/8.

  • Henry verifies every question and answer before publishing; the system holds no invented facts (fixtures here are labelled).
  • Copy celebrates participation: "Thanks for being part of this", never "You know her best".

Buttons

Button and IconButton. Gold primary with dark text is the default CTA; blue plays, teal confirms, coral cancels/closes, berry sells premium. One lg button per screen.

<Button variant="play" icon={<GameIcon kind="play" outline={false} shadow={false} size={20} />}>Level 22</Button>

HUD

CurrencyPill, ProgressBar, TimerChip, the dark inset kit that sits over the world. Two or three pills max in the top strip, right-aligned; glyphs overlap the left edge.

21

Panels

Panel is every modal. Every header is a chunky plate that straddles the panel's top edge, with outlined display type. Pick it by mood: banner for utility and level dialogs (blue; accent="teal" in Babe's flow), ribbon, gold with folded tails and stars, for celebration, sign, a wooden plank with leaves, for world events and the daily reward. Tones: cream, wood, sky. Primary action goes in footer.

<Panel title="Settings" onClose={close}>…rows…</Panel>
<Panel title="Complete" eyebrow="Level 23" header="ribbon" footer={<Button variant="confirm" size="lg">OK</Button>}>…</Panel>
<Panel title="Daily Reward" header="sign" tone="wood" footer={<Button size="lg">Claim</Button>}>…</Panel>
<Panel title="Who sent this?" accent="teal" footer={<Button variant="confirm" size="lg">Continue</Button>}>…</Panel>
  • header banner · ribbon · sign · none   accent blue · gold · teal · green · berry · coral   tone cream · wood · sky   title eyebrow onClose (coral × on the corner) width 340 padding 20 footer
  • Always centre a Panel inside a fixed scrim (--bg-scrim); one panel per screen.

Map & board

LevelNode pucks for the trail, StarRating for results, Tile for board and grid events. One current puck per map; locked pucks ignore taps; keep 24px of grass between pucks.

<LevelNode number={1} state="complete" stars={3} onClick={open} />  <LevelNode number={4} state="current" />  <LevelNode number={5} state="locked" />
<StarRating value={3} size={44} arc animate />
<Tile variant="reward" content={<GameIcon kind="coin" size={30} />} active onClick={pick} label="Coin tile" />
  • LevelNode state locked · current · complete, stars 0–3, size 84.   StarRating value/max 3, arc, animate.
  • Tile variant grass · sand · stone · water · reward · hazard · start, content, active (gold glow), onClick. Lay tiles in a grid with 6–8px gaps so the sides read.

Rewards & shop

RewardCard for streak calendars and prize tracks, ItemCell for inventory and merge grids, ShopRow for purchasables (first row featured).

<RewardCard title="Day 3" icon={<GameIcon kind="gift" size={40} />} state="available" onClick={claim} />
<RewardCard title="Day 7" featured icon={<GameIcon kind="heart" size={44} />} amount="+50" state="locked" />
<ItemCell state="filled" content={<GameIcon kind="egg" size={44} />} count={1} selected onClick={select} />
<ShopRow tone="featured" icon={<GameIcon kind="coins" size={48} />} title="3,000 coins" subtitle="One-time offer" price="USD 3.99" tag={<Tag tone="gold">3x the value</Tag>} />

  • ShopRow tone default · featured, price renders the buy button, tag sits on the top-right edge, action replaces the button.

Forms

Switch and Slider, the only inputs a casual game needs. Pair each with a GameIcon and a 16px bold label on the left; group rows in a white inset card inside the Settings panel.

<Switch checked={sound} onChange={setSound} label="Sound" />   <Switch checked tone="blue" size="sm" />
<Slider value={music} onChange={setMusic} icon={<GameIcon kind="music" />} label="Music" width="100%" />
  • Switch tone teal · blue · gold · green · berry, size sm · md, disabled.   Slider min/max/step, tone, icon, width; wraps a native range input.

Feedback

Badge pins a “!” or count to a corner, Tag labels merchandise and state, Toast confirms a pickup without a dialog (auto-dismiss ≈1.7s, anchored away from the thing just tapped).

<Badge count={3} style={{ position: 'absolute', top: -6, right: -6 }} />   <Badge />   <Badge count="NEW" tone="gold" size="sm" />
<Tag>Most popular</Tag>  <Tag tone="teal">3x the value</Tag>  <Tag tone="stone" icon={<i className="ph-fill ph-lock" />}>Locked</Tag>
<Toast icon={<GameIcon kind="coin" size={32} />} message="+50" detail="Coins collected" />
  • Tag tones by meaning: coral scarcity · teal/green value · gold new · berry premium · stone locked · blue limited · wood event.
  • Toast tone colours the headline; visible false renders nothing.
26

Voice & content

Short, warm, imperative, second person. Display caps for actions and titles; sentence case for everything else; numbers instead of descriptions.

CLAIM · OK · PLAY · SPIN · GO

Actions: one word, display caps. Never “Click here”, “Submit”, “Continue”.

LEVEL 23 · DAILY REWARD · x2 BOOST

Titles: noun phrases, ≤ 3 words. Numbers stay numerals.

Pick your daily gift!

Prompts: second person, sentence case, one exclamation mark per screen at most.

+500  ·  3/6  ·  00:02

Show the delta, the fraction, the countdown, don’t describe them.

You will still be able to watch rewarded videos.

Fine print: plain sentences, 12–14px, muted ink.

Warmful Valley · Day 3 · Level 22

Places and progress are proper nouns. No emoji anywhere; glyphs are GameIcons.
