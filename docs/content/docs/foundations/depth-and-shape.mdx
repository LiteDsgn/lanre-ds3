## The bevel

Every pressable thing is built the same way.

- A gradient face, from the 300 step to the 500 step by 42%.
- A hard `0 Npx 0` shadow in the 700 step. That's the bottom edge. It's 3, 5 or 7px for small, medium and large.
- A soft brown drop shadow below it.
- A 2px white line inset along the top.

Pressing moves the element down 4px and collapses the edge to 1px, over 120ms. Hover only brightens it by 6%. Press these to feel it.

Level pucks are ellipses with the same recipe, plus two white gloss spots. Tracks and cells go the other way. They're inset, in a dark translucent brown or green with an inner shadow.

## The step puck

A small numbered puck with the same bevel. The contributor trail, the memory blocks and the world signs all use it. Add `mp-puck` to any element, then `mp-puck-done` or `mp-puck-current` for the state, and `mp-puck-lg` for the bigger size.

## Shadows and glows

Shadows are tinted brown, never grey. A glow ring marks the thing you can tap or the thing you've won.

<TokenTable file="effects" prefix={['--highlight', '--shadow', '--glow']} />

## Corners

Everything is rounded.

- 6px for tags, 10px for badges and knobs.
- 16px for buttons, cells and cards, 22px for rows and tabs.
- 28px for panels, and a full pill for tracks and HUD pills.
- Pucks and decorative blobs are 50% ellipses.

<TokenTable file="effects" prefix="--radius" />

## Borders

- Panels have a 4px border in the next darker cream. Wood panels use 6px in `--wood-500`.
- Cells have a 3px translucent white border.
- Badges and tokens have a 2 to 3px solid white ring.
- There are no 1px hairlines.

<TokenTable file="effects" prefix="--border" />

## Transparency

Translucent surfaces only appear over the world. HUD pills are 82% brown, inventory cells 30% green, and the scrim 55%. There's no backdrop blur anywhere.
