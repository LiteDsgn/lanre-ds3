## Faces

- **Titan One** is the display face. It has one weight and covers titles, numbers and button labels. It's always uppercase with +0.02em tracking.
- **Quicksand** is the UI face, at 500, 600 and 700. It covers rows, body text and captions.

## Outlined text

Text that sits straight on the world is outlined. A white fill, a dark stroke painted behind it, and a hard drop. The stroke is 0.16em, or 0.20em under 20px.

The stroke follows the surface. Ink on grass, blue on blue level nodes, stone on locked ones, wood on planks.

## Casing

- Display type is made uppercase by CSS. Write it in Title Case in the source.
- UI type is sentence case.
- Small labels like SCORE, REWARD and LANGUAGE are UI caps with 0.08 to 0.12em tracking.

## Scale

The smallest text is 11px. Button labels are 14px or more.

Memory text is 20px with a 1.6 line height on iPad, and 17px on phones. The author’s summit letter is 22px with a 1.65 line height.
