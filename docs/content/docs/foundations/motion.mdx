## How things move

- **Arrivals bounce.** Stars, toasts and panels use `--ease-bounce` over 420ms, through the `mp-pop` keyframes.
- **Presses ease out** over 120ms.
- **Claimable things float** gently, with `mp-float` over 2.4s.
- **The current level and reachable tiles pulse** a ring, with `mp-pulse-ring` over 1.6s.
- **Progress fills** animate their width over 420ms.

There are no fade-only transitions and no parallax.

## States

- **Pressed** moves down and collapses the edge.
- **Disabled and locked** switch to stone at 85% opacity. Never use a greyscale filter on a button. RewardCard is the one exception, because its prize has to stay readable.
- **Selected** gets a teal border and glow.
- **Current** is blue with a pulse.
- **Complete** is blue with a row of stars.

## Reduced motion

When someone asks for reduced motion, all motion collapses. Card flips become swaps.
