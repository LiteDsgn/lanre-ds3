Allowed before reveal

  • Avatar mystery (the neutral “?”), “Mystery memory”, level number
  • The contributor's own optional clue, verbatim
  • Media titled neutrally: “Voice note”, “Photo 2”, “Video”
  • Up to three real names as choices, the correct one among them
  • The world the memory belongs to

Never before reveal

  • Name, relationship, portrait or generated avatar of the sender
  • Gemstone, score, rank or tier of anyone
  • Filenames, upload dates, device metadata, EXIF, alt text from filenames
  • Sender data in the DOM “hidden” with CSS, it must not be in the payload
  • Anything that spoils late arrivals or unrevealed people in Your People
The suitability flag is honest, not automatic: media that shows a face or says a name goes to a matching game or direct reveal instead of a false mystery.
