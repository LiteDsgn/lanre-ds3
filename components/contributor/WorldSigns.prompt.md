World signs: the road the signs hang over. Put `WorldSign`s inside it.

```jsx
<WorldSigns label="Where on the road">…signs…</WorldSigns>
```

- It draws a straight stretch of road behind the signs, in the road colours of the world it sits in. Put it inside a `data-world`, such as Base Camp.
- Give it a `label`. It's a group, and the label is what a screen reader announces.
