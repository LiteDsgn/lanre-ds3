## The package

Each level is a package of items from one contributor: text, photos, audio and video.

- Items play in the order the contributor chose.
- Nothing moves on by itself, and media never cuts off.
- Every player has pause and seek.
- Finishing is an explicit Continue. The recipient doesn't have to watch everything first.

The package opens in a [RoadsideViewer](/docs/adventure-components/viewer/roadside-viewer), a wooden cabinet over the map. Its screen plays one piece at a time, and one key per piece lets the recipient move around.

## The guess

- A wrong guess turns coral with a ×, and the retry stays open.
- "Just tell me" is always there.
- A right guess turns green with a ✓, and the others dim. Then the face on the [RevealTray](/docs/adventure-components/viewer/reveal-tray) flips to the friend.

## Other ways in

Each level uses one interaction, chosen once and saved: guess, match or direct reveal. Media that gives the sender away gets a matching game or a direct reveal.
