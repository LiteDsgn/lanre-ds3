## Does it give you away?

Contributors say honestly whether their package shows who they are. That decides whether the level is a mystery or a direct reveal.

## Uploads

- Upload tiles keep their order, and that's the order Babe sees them in.
- A failed upload blocks submitting until it's fixed or removed.
- Filenames only ever show on the contributor's side.

## One main memory

Each contributor has one main-path memory. Anything else they send becomes a discovery beside the road.
