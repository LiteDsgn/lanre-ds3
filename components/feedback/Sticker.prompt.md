Sticker: a round glyph badge that gives a line of copy a small visual anchor.

```jsx
<Sticker icon="heart" tone="berry" />
<Sticker icon="warning" tone="coral" weight="bold" />
```

- It is hidden from screen readers, so never let it carry meaning alone.
- `tone` picks a colour family (its 300, 500 and 700 shades make the highlight, face and side). Prefer `Notice` when the sticker sits beside a message.
