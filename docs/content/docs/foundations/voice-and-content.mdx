## Voice

The game talks to one player, never about itself. It's warm, short and direct. "Pick your daily gift!" There's no "we".

## Words on screen

- **Actions are one word**, in display caps. CLAIM, OK, PLAY, SPIN, SHOP, GO. Never "Click here", "Submit" or "Continue".
- **Titles are noun phrases** of three words or fewer. LEVEL 23, DAILY REWARD, COMPLETE, x2 BOOST.
- **Prompts and rows are sentence case** in the UI face. "Sound", "Vibration", "One-time offer".
- **One exclamation mark per screen**, at most.
- **Fine print** is plain sentences at 12 to 14px in muted ink.
- **Places and progress are proper nouns.** Warmful Valley, Pine Ridge, Day 3, Level 22.
- **Merchandising labels** are one to three uppercase words in a Tag. MOST POPULAR, 3X THE VALUE, NEW.
- **No emoji, ever.** Glyphs are painted icons.

## Numbers

Show the number instead of describing it. A delta, a fraction or a countdown: `+500`, `3/6`, `00:02`, `75%`.

- **Currency** uses k and M with comma groups. `50,253k`, `1.2M`, `2,720`.
- **Scores** use thin-space groups. `14 000`.
- **Timers** are `mm:ss` or `4h 12m`.
- **Prices** show the currency code, not a symbol. `USD 0.99`.

## In the Birthday Adventure

The author speaks to the recipient directly, in the second person. It's warm, playful and brief, and it's never a test.

- Use "Just tell me", "Try again", "Keep exploring" and "Continue". These override the one-word action rule above.
- Don't invent jokes on the author’s behalf, or a finished letter.
- In the recipient’s flow, the teal button is the default for continue and confirm. Gold is kept for rewards and the summit.

For contributors, say plainly that this is a surprise shown to the recipient. Show them what stays anonymous and what gets revealed.
