## The set

The icons are [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com), in two weights. A no-build page gets them from a CDN through `tokens/icons.css`. An app can install `@phosphor-icons/web` and import the fill and bold styles instead. This is a stand-in. The reference games use painted icons, and Phosphor's filled weight is the closest match to their chunky shapes. Swap in painted art in `assets/` when it exists.

## Treatment

`GameIcon` draws a filled glyph in a tone, with a dark outline painted behind it and a hard drop. Semantic kinds fix the glyph and the tone together. A coin is always a gold coin, a gem a blue gem, a heart coral, energy teal, a gift berry, and a lock stone.

## GameIcon or plain Phosphor

Use `GameIcon` for a glyph that stands on its own, like one on the world, on a panel or on a medal.

A glyph inside another component is plain Phosphor. That means a `Button`, `IconButton` or `Tag` icon slot, or a level node. The component already styles it, so pass `<i className="ph-fill ph-star" />` and leave the rest to it.

Never rebuild the sticker look in CSS. If a glyph needs it, it's a `GameIcon`.

## Weights

- **Fill** for pictures. Coins, stars, hearts, gifts and chests.
- **Bold** for utility strokes. ×, +, ✓, carets and the gear.

Inside a bevelled button the glyph is plain white, with no outline or shadow. The button already has the depth.

## Sizes

Icons come in 20, 28, 40 and 56px. Currency glyphs overlap the left edge of their pill.

## Never

- No icon fonts other than Phosphor.
- No emoji.
- No unicode characters as icons.

There's no logo yet. Wherever a mark would go, the name is set in outlined display type.
