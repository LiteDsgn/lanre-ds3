## Does it give you away?

Contributors say honestly whether their memory shows who they are. That decides whether the level is a mystery or a direct reveal. The two answers sit side by side as tall cards.

## Memory blocks

A memory is a stack of blocks: words, photos, a video or a voice note, in any mix. Each one is an `UploadTile`, joined to the one above by a short stretch of road.

- Blocks keep their order, and that's the order the recipient sees them in. Contributors move them with the up and down buttons on each block.
- Every upload shows its state: uploading, done, failed, stalled or missing.
- A failed or missing upload blocks sending until it's fixed or removed.
- Filenames only ever show on the contributor's side.

## One main memory

Each contributor has one main-path memory. Anything else they send becomes a discovery beside the road.
