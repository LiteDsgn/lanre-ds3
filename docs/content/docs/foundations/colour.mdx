## One recipe for every ramp

Each tone runs from 100 to 900, and the steps always do the same job.

- **500** is the face.
- **300** is the top highlight.
- **700** is the bevel, the solid edge under anything pressable.
- **900** is the outline.

## What each tone is for

- **Meadow green** is the environment. Grass, bushes and board tiles.
- **Sky blue** is action and "current". Play buttons, the current level and water.
- **Gold** is reward and the main call to action. Coins, stars and Claim. Gold buttons take dark brown text, never white.
- **Coral** is danger and urgency. Hearts and close buttons too.
- **Teal** is confirm, OK and value.
- **Berry** is premium, boosts and hazards.
- **Wood** is frames, fences, signs and the bottom bar.
- **Stone** is anything locked or disabled. It replaces the tone completely.
- **Parchment** creams are panel surfaces.
- **Ink** is a warm brown. Text is never black.
